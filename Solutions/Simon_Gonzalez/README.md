# Simon Gonzalez

I am a researcher in the High Energy Physics group at the Universidad de los Andes, Colombia. I am currently working with my colleagues to use deep learning models to solve complex inverse problems in phase-contrast X-ray imaging, and we are now venturing into scattering muography of civil engineering structures. 

My research interests focus on the use of artificial intelligence to overcome the analytical and numerical limitations we encounter daily in physics. Specifically, I am interested in X-ray imaging, medical physics, and structural analysis using noninvasive techniques. I am also interested in nuclear physics and the transition to clean energy.

I attended the HSF Machine Learning mini course to better understand the basic concepts of machine learning that we sometimes take for granted; with this knowledge, many models are no longer black boxes and become more interpretable.

I consider myself good at data analysis and exploratory techniques in Python, but not so good at remembering the syntax of the libraries.

I'm looking forward to connecting with members of the scientific community who share similar interests to mine, so we can share knowledge and collaborate on projects.
